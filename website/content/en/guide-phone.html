The parent panel normally opens only on the computer itself. Turn on phone access and the same panel opens on your phone too: at home, at work or on the go. Give time, lock the screen, answer a request or watch the screen live.

It uses the free Tailscale app, which builds an encrypted, private network between your phone and the computer that only you can use. Nothing needs changing on your router, and no door is opened to the internet.

{% call m.callout('tip', 'Did it in the setup wizard?') %}

Step 8 of the first-run setup is these same four steps. If you finished it there, the panel already opens on your phone; come back here only to turn it on later or switch it off.

{% endcall %}

Phone access in four steps

Do these at the computer, in the panel. It takes about five minutes in all.

    In the panel go to Settings → Access from your phone and press Turn on phone access. BioKids installs Tailscale on the computer by itself; there is nothing for you to download.

Everyday use

To open the panel on your phone, the Tailscale app there just needs to be connected. After 30 minutes without any action the panel signs you out, and you enter your PIN again.

You can do almost everything from the phone: give +10 min, lock or shut down from Today, answer Requests, look at History and Insights, watch the screen live. Only Switch user works at the computer alone.

{% call m.callout('privacy', 'What this means for privacy') %}

Tailscale's coordination server is the one outside party, and only once you turn this on; BioKids runs Tailscale without its logging (--no-logs-no-support). The panel itself travels only over your own encrypted network and never through a BioKids server. One thing worth knowing: because of the secure (HTTPS) certificate, your computer's .ts.net name appears in public certificate logs. That is a name only, nothing about your family. Every outbound connection BioKids makes is on the Privacy page.

{% endcall %} {% call m.callout('danger', 'Do not forward ports') %}

Do not set up port forwarding on your router to reach the panel, and do not turn on Tailscale Funnel. BioKids refuses Funnel anyway: the panel is open only to the devices on your own network, and should stay that way.

{% endcall %}

Turning phone access off

Go to Settings → Access from your phone and press Turn off phone access. The panel no longer opens from your phone. Tailscale stays installed on the computer; to turn access back on, press the same button again.

Something wrong?

The panel will not open on my phone

Open the Tailscale app on the phone and check it says "Connected". Make sure the computer is on and online; a sleeping or switched-off computer cannot be reached. Check that the phone and the computer are signed in to the same Tailscale account.

Does Tailscale cost anything?

No. A free personal Tailscale account is enough for a family's use like this.

Can my partner use it from their phone too?

Yes. Install Tailscale on their phone and sign in with the same account; in the panel, give them their own parent account with their name and PIN under Settings → Parents. Up to four parents are allowed.

What if I lose my phone?

The panel still asks for the PIN, so whoever finds the phone cannot get in without it. Even so, remove that phone from your network in the device list of Tailscale's admin console, on their website.
