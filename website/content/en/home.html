A safe computer for your child. Your rules, and everything stays home.

BioKids is a free, open-source parental-control system for children aged 4–10, built on Debian 13. It brings screen-time limits, safe browsing, a live view of the screen on your phone, and an AI assistant that runs on the computer itself. No account, and nothing is sent to a cloud.

👁️ Live view

Your child's screen, right now, on your phone

Press Watch live in the panel and see your child's screen at about one picture a second. At home or at work, it makes no difference.

  • The child is told. While you watch, their screen says "👁 Mum can see your screen now". That sign cannot be turned off.
  • Nothing is recorded. Each picture replaces the last in memory; leave the page and the watching ends.
  • On the record. Every time watching starts, it is written in the audit log.

🤖 Ask Me · on-device AI

An assistant for curious kids that never goes to the cloud

The child types a question or asks it aloud, and gets a short answer for their age, in the language they asked in. The answer comes from a model running on the computer itself.

  • Fully offline: the Gemma language model (llama.cpp), Whisper for speech, and an offline Wikipedia to check the answers.
  • It refuses what a child should not ask, and you see what it refused. Ordinary questions are not kept.
  • The microphone listens only while the button is held, for up to 15 seconds, and nothing is recorded.
  • It is optional: off until you download it in the panel and turn it on for a child. It needs 7 GB of memory or more.

⏱️ Time rules

Screen time: a few clear settings, the rest takes care of itself

Choose the daily minutes and the hours they can use the computer, for weekdays and weekends. BioKids counts, warns and enforces.

  • Zio and Sio say it aloud when 10, 5 and 1 minutes are left.
  • When time is up, lock the screen, log out or shut the computer down: your choice.
  • Optional breaks, eye exercises, and time earned by learning.
  • Holidays, birthdays: separate rules for special days.

📊 History and insights

A year at a glance, any day at a tap

The Today screen shows each child's time left; History and Insights show where the time went.

  • A year-long heatmap, the last 14 days as bars, and every day in detail: apps, sites opened, requests.
  • Insights: the week's highlights, time by kind of app, and the hours of the day it was used.
  • An optional weekly summary by email on Sunday evening, sent from your own mail server.

🛡️ Safe browsing

The internet open, the worst of it closed

Adult, gambling, malware and scam sites are blocked by a filter on the computer, on the children's accounts only.

  • About 291,000 harmful domains blocked; SafeSearch and YouTube's restricted mode enforced.
  • Or, if you like, only the sites I choose.
  • On a blocked site the child presses Ask a grown-up; the request reaches your phone and you answer with one tap.
  • No proxy in the middle: BioKids never decrypts anyone's traffic.

🧒 The child's screen

No desktop, no settings: just big tiles

The child signs in by tapping their picture and lands in a launcher of the apps and sites you chose.

  • Tiles that read themselves aloud for children who cannot read yet; two looks, for 4–7 and 8–10.
  • Brightness at the bottom, volume at the top; open apps wait without starting over.
  • Learning corners for English with Sio and Turkish reading, and a surprise card every day.
  • GCompris, Tux Paint, Scratch, Chrome Music Lab and more than 30 ready apps and sites.

{{ m.head('principles', 'Principles that do not change', 'Promise', 'These are written into the project\'s constitution; no feature may break them.', 'indigo') }}

🏠 Nothing leaves the device

No telemetry, no analytics, no cloud inference. Every outbound connection is listed in the open.

👀 The child knows

No hidden screenshots, no keylogging. Supervision is always visible.

🔒 Fail safe, not silent

If protection breaks, the session locks and you are told. Never "continue unprotected".

🧯 Never locks you out

Anything that could lock you out is optional, reversible and documented.

📴 Offline first

Every core feature works with the network cable unplugged.

🙂 A panel anyone understands

A setting that needs a paragraph of explanation lives behind "Advanced".
