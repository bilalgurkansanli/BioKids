Once BioKids is installed, a setup wizard opens by itself the first time you sign in with your parent account. In nine short steps you choose your PIN, add your first child and set their rules. It opens again at every sign-in until it is finished, and it never opens in a child's session.

{% call m.callout('tip', 'Before you start') %}

Have a pen and paper ready: in step 3 you will write down a recovery code that is shown only once. Step 1 of 9 at the top of the page tells you where you are.

{% endcall %}
    {% call m.step('Choose the language', 'language') %}

    The language of the panel and of the child's screen: Türkçe or English. The box below it is BioKids' promise: Nothing leaves your device. Press Continue.

    Your name and a 6-digit PIN: you will use it every time you open the panel. Type it again in the second box. PINs that are easy to guess, such as 123456 or 111111, are refused.

    {{ m.shot(lang ~ '/wizard-2-parent', 'Step 2 of the setup wizard: boxes for your name, a 6-digit PIN and the PIN again.', 'Step 2 of 9: Parent account.', sizes='(min-width: 960px) 760px, 100vw') }} {% endcall %} {% call m.step('Write down the passphrase and the recovery code', 'passphrase') %}

    First choose a Passphrase of at least 10 characters. It is separate from the PIN and is asked for the big things: turning protection off, removing a child or a parent, and backing up.

    Below it is Your recovery code: 8 words. It is the easiest way back in if you forget your PIN, and it is never shown again. The words are Turkish words written without Turkish letters (such as sifir), even in English. Write them on paper, then tick I have written the recovery code down.

    {{ m.shot(lang ~ '/wizard-3-passphrase', 'Step 3 of the setup wizard: a Passphrase box, an eight-word recovery code and the I have written the recovery code down box.', 'Step 3 of 9: Passphrase and recovery code. These words are an example; yours will differ.', sizes='(min-width: 960px) 760px, 100vw') }} {% call m.callout('warn', 'Do not keep the code as a photo of the screen') %}

    Write it on paper and put it somewhere safe. Anyone who knows the code can change your PIN.

    {% endcall %} {% endcall %} {% call m.step('Add your first child', 'child') %}

    Their name, year of birth, a username on the computer (lowercase letters and digits only, e.g. elif), a picture for the login screen, and the language of their screen.

    The year of birth decides the look of their screen: up to age 7, bigger tiles that read themselves aloud (the 4–7 look); after that, more tiles, a clock and their own files (the 8–10 look). You can add your other children later in the panel.

    {{ m.shot(lang ~ '/wizard-4-child', 'Step 4 of the setup wizard: name, year of birth, username on the computer, a choice of animal pictures and the language.', 'Step 4 of 9: Add a child.', sizes='(min-width: 960px) 760px, 100vw') }} {% endcall %} {% call m.step('Choose the apps', 'apps') %}

    Choose which tiles your child will see. The ones that suit their age come ticked: GCompris, Paint, Numbers, Music Lab, English and others. Volume, brightness and the shut-down button always stay on the screen. You can change all of this later in the panel.

    {{ m.shot(lang ~ '/wizard-5-apps', 'Step 5 of the setup wizard: a grid of app and site boxes, some ticked and some not.', 'Step 5 of 9: Apps. From this step to step 8 there is also a "Skip for now" button.', sizes='(min-width: 960px) 760px, 100vw', cls='crop-top') }} {% endcall %} {% call m.step('Set the daily time', 'time') %}

    BioKids suggests a starting point by age: for 4–7, 60 minutes on weekdays and 90 at weekends; for 8–10, 120 and 180. In Allowed hours type something like 16:00-20:00, or leave it empty to allow the whole day.

    Choose what happens When time runs out: Lock the screen, Log out, or Shut the computer down (the default). More rules are in the time rules guide.

Where is the panel?

The parent panel is the BioKids Parent Panel icon on your desktop and in the applications menu, and it opens by itself when you sign in with your parent account (you can turn that off in Settings). If you turned on phone access, it is on your phone too. The panel's Help page answers the common questions, even offline.

Something wrong?

The wizard did not open.

The wizard opens only when you sign in with your parent account. If it did not, double-click the BioKids Parent Panel icon on the desktop; the panel takes you to the step where you left off.

My child is not on the login screen.

The computer needs one restart after setup. If they are still missing, check that your child is listed on the panel's Children screen.

I filled in a step wrongly.

No problem: time, apps, internet and voices can all be changed later in the panel. Add or edit children on the Children screen.

I forgot to write down the recovery code.

As long as you know your PIN, nothing is lost. If you forget the PIN too, type sudo biokids-admin reset-pin in a terminal on the computer to set a new one: Troubleshooting.
